# Delayed requirement checks — 17 October 2026

A newly completed independent exit check attached to an exact study target schedules a check for 24 hours later. Learn displays one due action, **Try a delayed check**, or resumes an unfinished delayed check. It requests a different source-supported question for that same target and kind, excluding the earlier diagnostic, practice and exit questions. Saved review sessions retain their source, exact target ID, triggering session, scheduled/start times, draft, attempts, help and provider/model provenance. Previously saved sessions remain compatible; old records without a due date do not receive an invented historic schedule.

Immediate and delayed evidence are queried separately. A chapter argument or unrelated application cannot become a delayed proof result. An unassisted correct delayed answer schedules another check after three days; helped, unsuccessful or uncertain delayed attempts schedule a return after one day. These intervals are a simple product policy, not a model of memory. Help stays recorded when the learner returns to teaching or practice inside a delayed session. An uncertain assessment stays uncertain, rather than being interpreted as failure or success.

## Evidence and limits

The view model accepts a controllable clock; explicit due dates and attempt timestamps persist. The course view refreshes its due-time check each minute and on appearance. Three new regression methods cover each concept/proof/application kind at the due boundary, exact question/target identity, restart with an unsent draft and active review, separate immediate evidence, helped/uncertain/partial results, next due dates, repeated-question rejection and source-safety gates. The 18 October follow-up retained full macOS run has 266 passing tests (`/tmp/cauchy-learning-completion-audit.xcresult`), with no failed or skipped tests. No live model generation was needed for these tests.

The fresh-question validator enforces structure, kind, absence of hints, non-repetition and current source; a model can still produce a weak or semantically similar question. A correct delayed answer is one observation on one problem, not a retention or mastery guarantee. The [isolated native walkthrough](learning-ui-fixture.md) exercised a seeded due application review, a new exact-target question, unsent draft, quit/cold restart, keyboard submission and an uncertain result. Persisted metadata retained its triggering session, due/start times and one-day return; the UI showed uncertainty without credit and returned to one next action. Offline authored assessment does not establish live question quality. Local checkpoint references are recorded in [work tracking](../work-tracking.md); a checkpoint is not a published release.
