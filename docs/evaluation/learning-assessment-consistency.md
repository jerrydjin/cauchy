# Criteria consistency across question kinds

Tracked by [JERRY-89](https://linear.app/jerryjin/issue/JERRY-89). The assessment gate previously checked rubric contradictions only for proof questions. Concept and application questions can also have essential grading criteria; an optimistic overall grade must not override a missing or uncertain criterion.

`LearningAssessmentPolicy` is now the shared pure policy for returned tutor assessments, saved-evidence eligibility and diagnostic/practice advancement. Every supplied rubric requires complete, unique indexed assessments and usable feedback. Non-proof rubrics allow one to eight criteria; proofs retain their stricter three-to-eight requirement. Non-proof questions without a rubric retain holistic grading. Any returned criterion uncertainty makes the effective result uncertain; a correct grade with a not-met criterion becomes partial. Met criteria never promote an originally partial/incorrect/uncertain judgement. Malformed coverage is rejected rather than guessed.

Provider instructions now request every supplied criterion for every question kind. The existing source-validity, help and attempt rules remain intact. A concept/application practice result with contradictory criteria cannot unlock an independent exit. A failed/uncertain delayed check earns neither independent credit nor plan advancement, and receives the existing one-day retry interval rather than the three-day successful delayed interval.

Historical records are not migrated or rewritten. Original grades, answers, criterion feedback, help and scheduling remain intact, but unsupported saved correct grades no longer count as independent evidence. Their lesson/path/history presentations say **Assessment needs checking**; history separately shows the original recorded grade. Legacy practice resumes with revision rather than bypassing the gate through its raw correct grade. Proof-specific incomplete-assessment labels are retained. The history fallback for an unmatched criterion displays its recorded index without incrementing an unchecked integer.
