# Questions about chapter feedback — 23 October 2026

[JERRY-112](https://linear.app/jerryjin/issue/JERRY-112) extends **Ask about this feedback** to assessed main chapter arguments and their completed results. It reuses the existing [feedback discussion](learning-feedback.md) rather than creating another chat surface. The exact saved attempt supplies the complete question, learner argument, original assessment, next step and visible criteria. Chapter/refresher keys, hints and future questions are excluded. Unanswered chapter prompts and prerequisite refresher feedback do not become main-argument feedback contexts.

Discussion identity remains stable when moving to the result or personally setting an assessment aside. A reply cannot regrade or reinstate the result, change its help record, create topic credit or change its schedule. Source validation, busy-state protection and cancellation retain the existing clarification path. No schema, provider instruction or grading-policy change is needed.

During a fresh chapter argument, earlier replies are available under **Help with this argument → Saved feedback discussions**. These panels are titled **Saved feedback discussion**, retain any unsent follow-up as read-only text and offer **Return to my answer**. They have no editor or Ask action. Reading a saved reply records help on the current answer while retaining the exact new draft and earlier assessment. Returning focuses the current answer editor. The help disclosure uses the complete assistance state, including saved discussion help, rather than labelling every form of assistance as a prerequisite refresher.
