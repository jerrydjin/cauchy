# Focused review and chapter actions

Tracked by [JERRY-77](https://linear.app/jerryjin/issue/JERRY-77). This continues the hierarchy work on baseline `79ded1c`: long titles in delayed reviews and chapter recommendations could still push the primary action down, while chapter introductions and refresher guidance competed with the question.

## Presentation

Delayed reviews, ready chapters and saved chapters now use two-line titles with full accessibility labels. Existing contextual disclosures hold the full titles, learning goal or connection, and involved ideas. A ready chapter leads with its chapter title rather than a concatenation of every topic name. Generic coaching no longer sits above its action.

The active chapter shows its category, compact title, full question and reasoning editor. Challenge details contains its complete chapter/challenge titles, connection, original source quotations and page actions. Help with this argument contains the prerequisite refresher and its consequences; its collapsed label retains the refresher-used status. The independent-check instruction is attached to the outside-help checkbox beside submission, instead of being repeated above every exit question. Source-open reporting remains beside submission.

These are Learn view changes. No eligibility, scheduling, tutoring, source, grading or persistence policy changed. Full arguments and quotations remain available in context.
