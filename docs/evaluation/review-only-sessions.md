# Review-only study sessions

Tracked by [JERRY-79](https://linear.app/jerryjin/issue/JERRY-79). The existing on-demand plan now offers **Review and learn** (the default) and **Review only**. Review-only plans take up to two or three distinct delayed requirements from the existing scheduling policy: saved delayed checks first, then due-time order. They retain exact concept/proof/application targets. A short queue stays short; an empty queue explains that no delayed checks are due and disables Start. Future checks are not brought forward and new lessons are never used to fill the plan.

The course retains its single recommendation. Sequencing explanations remain inside the preview's disclosure. Plans use the existing item modes, source blockers, fresh-question generation, pause/resume, progress and outcome records; no persistence schema, grading policy, reader behavior or provider configuration changes are needed. A saved draft with changed source remains an actionable source-review stop. The mixed plan retains one delayed check followed by new work.
