# Explicit syllabus reconciliation — 17 October 2026

**Compare with your syllabus** accepts required items, one per line, with their concept/proof/application obligations and a provenance note. Each item receives a stable ID. For each kind of work, the learner records a mapping to one or more exact source targets, marks the material absent from the supplied notes, or leaves the mapping unresolved. Every change appends a dated decision, note and target/objective/source snapshot. Previous decisions remain inspectable. These are user-reviewed mappings; the app does not infer a curriculum or certify absence from all possible sources.

Source coverage counts only mappings whose targets still exist, match their saved kind/objective/source and correction revisions, and have no unresolved source obligations. Missing proofs cannot be replaced with concept or application targets. A corrected source or regenerated target can make a mapping unresolved without erasing the required syllabus item or its history. Course reordering and source-inventory regeneration retain the syllabus. New source inventory never auto-resolves an earlier absence finding.

Coverage and learner evidence are separate counts. A source mapping earns no learning credit. The learner-evidence count requires an independent correct immediate check for every exact mapped requirement; delayed checks remain separate. Neither count establishes whole-subject mastery or retention.
